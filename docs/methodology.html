Methodology

How forecasts are scored, what counts as an irregularity, and how theories get tested — before anyone claims a result.

1. Collect

Polls, ratings, and market prices from verified sources only. Every row of data carries its source URL.

2. Forecast

Poll averages, fundamentals, and market-implied probabilities compete on equal footing with pre-registered rules.

3. Backtest

Score against certified outcomes (FEC, House Clerk, NARA): Brier, log-loss, calibration — with small-n warnings.

4. Flag

Extreme misses, stale forecasts, and missing outcomes are logged publicly, never silently patched.

Scoring metrics

MetricWhat it rewardsWatch out
Brier score (lower better)Probabilities close to outcomesNeeds shared race sets for fair comparison
Log-loss (lower better)Honest uncertainty; punishes confident errorsVery sensitive to 0/100 calls
Accuracy @ 0.5Correct binary callsIgnores confidence; coin-flips look okay
Calibration"70% forecasts happen ~70% of the time"Needs volume per bin to mean anything
BiasDetects systematic leanConfounded by the cycle's base rate

Implemented in scripts/backtest.py. Run python scripts/backtest.py --demo-synthetic for a labeled pipeline test.
